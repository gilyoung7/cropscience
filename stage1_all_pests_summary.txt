Stage-1 Cascade (sheath_blight only)
==================================================================

A two-stage cascade was previously evaluated on top of Stage1 to lower the
false-alert rate while keeping recall high. Stage1a (=Stage1) raises an alert
at tau_a; Stage1b (XGB, max_depth=4, n_est=200) re-scores the alert window and
keeps it only if its score >= tau_b (chosen on val by F1).

Variants
- v1 no-monitor:  Stage1b uses only the 211-dim tabular feats at the alert row
                  + the Stage1a score (1 dim).
- v2 M=7/14/21:   v1 + a post-alert monitoring window of M days
                  (per-channel mean/std/slope/max/min/Δ → 6×D + 1 = 181 dims).
